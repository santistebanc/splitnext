Splitnext: five designs that hold up

Third round. Each direction is drawn against the hard cases instead of a tidy four-person trip:

  • a 14-person group, Interrail 2026, with 126 expenses over three months
  • an expense two people paid, split unevenly across 13 of the 14 (a hostel split by nights)
  • an itemized dinner bill, and a settle-up plan of 10 payments

It keeps the round-two rules: flat, full width, small corners, no repeated text.
